MISSISSIPPI LEGISLATURE

2026 1st Extraordinary Session

To: Rules

By: Representative Bell (65th)

House Resolution 2

(As Adopted by House)

A RESOLUTION MOURNING THE LOSS AND COMMEMORATING THE LIFE, EXTRAORDINARY LEGACY OF PUBLIC SERVICE, AND DISTINGUISHED LEGISLATIVE CAREER OF THE HONORABLE WILLIAM R. "BO" BROWN, AND EXPRESSING DEEPEST SYMPATHY TO HIS FAMILY AND FRIENDS UPON HIS PASSING.

     WHEREAS, it is written in Psalm 73:26, "My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever," and as such, the immaculate author and finisher of our souls' destiny summoned the mortal presence of the Honorable William R. "Bo" Brown, beloved husband, father, statesman, educator, community leader and distinguished member of the Mississippi House of Representatives, to eternal rest on June 8, 2026, rendering great sorrow and loss to his family, legislative colleagues, constituents and many friends; and

     WHEREAS, the City of Jackson, Hinds County and the State of Mississippi lost a revered public servant, respected voice of reason, steadfast advocate and faithful servant-leader with the passing of Representative Brown, and there is now a hush in our hearts as we come together to pay our respects to the memory of one who has been called to join that innumerable heavenly caravan; and

     WHEREAS, William R. "Bo" Brown was born on November 8, 1944, to the union of the late Willie Roy Brown and Bessie Brown, and throughout the entirety of his life embodied the principles of faith, scholarship, perseverance, service and uplift that would ultimately define his remarkable journey and enduring legacy; and

     WHEREAS, Representative Brown pursued academic excellence with diligence and distinction, earning a bachelor of arts degree in political science from Tennessee State University, a master of public policy and administration degree from Jackson State University, completing specialized studies in public administration and organizational management, and further broadening his educational horizons through postgraduate studies and certification at Santa Clara University in California; and

     WHEREAS, guided by a profound commitment to service, Representative Brown began his professional career as a teacher and coach in the Jackson Public School District, where from 1967 until 1971 he inspired young minds while teaching American History, Economics and American Government, serving as Assistant Football Coach, Junior Class Sponsor and School Social Committee Chairman, helping to shape the lives and futures of countless students; and

     WHEREAS, his passion for public service soon led him to positions of increasing responsibility within the federal government, first serving as a Community Relations Specialist with the United States Department of Justice during the implementation of the Civil Rights Act of 1968, where he worked to resolve social and civil conflicts and facilitate constructive dialogue between communities and governmental entities, before serving with distinction in numerous capacities within the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development from 1971 until 1983, including Community Services Advisor, Neighborhood and Consumer Affairs Representative and Equal Opportunity Specialist, administering programs that improved housing, community development and opportunities for disadvantaged families throughout Mississippi; and

     WHEREAS, Representative Brown later enjoyed a highly successful career in the private sector as an Insurance Agent, Field Underwriter and Financial Representative, serving with New York Life Insurance Company and Modern Woodmen of America for decades, earning numerous professional distinctions including recognition as a Quality Council Agent, National Sales Achievement recipient and National Quality Award honoree while assisting families and businesses with financial planning and security; and

     WHEREAS, possessing the gift of communication and community engagement, Representative Brown also served as a radio broadcaster on WOKJ and WKXI-FM, utilizing the power of media to inform, inspire and connect citizens throughout the Jackson metropolitan area; and

     WHEREAS, Representative Brown's devotion to public service culminated in his election to the Jackson City Council, where he faithfully represented the citizens of Ward Four from 1997 until 2005 and served as President of the Council on three separate occasions, earning a reputation as a thoughtful consensus-builder whose leadership was grounded in integrity, civility and a sincere desire to improve the quality of life for all residents of Jackson; and

     WHEREAS, continuing his lifelong commitment to civic engagement and representative government, Representative Brown was elected to the Mississippi House of Representatives in November 2019 and assumed office on January 7, 2020, representing House District 70 in Hinds County until his passing, during which time he faithfully served on numerous legislative committees, including Agriculture, Banking and Financial Services, Transportation, Judiciary B, Medicaid, Corrections, Tourism and Reapportionment, diligently advocating for the needs and interests of his constituents and the citizens of Mississippi; and

     WHEREAS, throughout his legislative tenure, Representative Brown championed issues affecting education, public safety, criminal justice reform, mental health services, infrastructure improvements, community development, public accountability and equal opportunity, while remaining a steadfast advocate for fully funding public education, supporting teachers and improving opportunities for future generations; and

     WHEREAS, Representative Brown represented a style of leadership increasingly rare in public life, earning admiration and respect from colleagues on both sides of the aisle through wisdom rather than volume, humility rather than self-promotion, and character rather than controversy, proving himself to be a steady and trusted voice whose influence extended far beyond the walls of the Mississippi Capitol; and

     WHEREAS, a man of deep and abiding faith, Representative Brown was a devoted member of New Mt. Zion Missionary Baptist Church, where he faithfully served and worshipped, drawing strength and guidance from his relationship with God throughout every season of his life; and

     WHEREAS, his commitment to service extended well beyond government through active membership and leadership in numerous civic, educational and fraternal organizations, including the Tennessee State University Alumni Association, Jackson State University Alumni Association, YMCA, BelAir Homeowners' Association, Jackson Association of Life Underwriters and Financial Planners, the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People, Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Incorporated, and Prince Hall Masonic Lodge No. 242 under the M.W. Stringer Grand Lodge of Mississippi; and

     WHEREAS, as a proud member of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Incorporated, Representative Brown exemplified the fraternity's cardinal principles of manhood, scholarship, perseverance and uplift through a lifetime devoted to empowering others, strengthening communities and serving with unwavering integrity and purpose; and

     WHEREAS, as legislative members of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Incorporated, serving in the Mississippi House of Representatives, who served alongside Representative William R. "Bo" Brown, we pause to honor our beloved Brother, his legislative work, his advocacy for his constituents, and his unwavering dedication to improving the lives of others, which demonstrated the intestinal fortitude of his strength of character, wisdom and perseverance that define our Brotherhood; and

     WHEREAS, because his leadership extended beyond the halls of government, uplifting communities and inspiring future generations to serve with integrity and purpose, we extend deepest condolences to his family, friends, constituents and all Brothers of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Incorporated, and Brother Brown has entered Omega Chapter, but his spirit, service and example will remain with us always - "Friendship is Essential to the Soul."; and

     WHEREAS, it is written in Psalm 90:12, "Teach us to number our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom," and Representative Brown's 81 years upon this earth stand as a testament to a life well-lived, richly blessed and abundantly poured into the service of others, leaving behind a treasury of wisdom, compassion and noble example for generations to come; and

     WHEREAS, Representative Brown's love and deeply felt respect for this hallowed institution was readily apparent in his professionalism, kindness and collegial spirit, and the members of the Mississippi House of Representatives will forever cherish the privilege of having served alongside a gentleman whose conduct reflected honor, dignity and genuine concern for the welfare of others; and

     WHEREAS, though he has now traversed the threshold separating the sanctum of mortal life from the immortality afforded by life's eternal ever after, the indelible imprint of Representative Brown's service, friendship and leadership will continue to resonate in the hearts of all who knew him, including:  his beloved wife of 54 years, Imelda Anderson Brown; daughters, Kidada Brown, Dawn Brown Thornton and Michelle Phillips; grandsons, Camden Lyon Thornton and Quinton Phillips; and a host of other relatives, colleagues, constituents and friends; and

     WHEREAS, it is written in John 14:27-28, "Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to youDo not let your hearts be troubled, and do not let them be afraid," and it is our prayer that these sacred words provide comfort, strength and assurance to all who mourn the earthly departure of Representative Brown while celebrating the eternal reward promised to the faithful; and

     WHEREAS, it is with sincere humility that the House of Representatives honors the monumental life, legacy and accomplishments of Representative William R. "Bo" Brown, whose unwavering commitment to faith, family, education, public service and community advancement illuminated the pathway of hope and opportunity for countless individuals, and whose example shall forever remain an enduring beacon of inspiration to the citizens of Mississippi:

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE STATE OF MISSISSIPPI, That we do hereby mourn the loss, commemorate the life and honor the extraordinary public service, distinguished legislative career and enduring legacy of the Honorable William R. "Bo" Brown, Representative of House District 70, and express deepest sympathy to his family and friends upon his passing.

     BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, That copies of this resolution be furnished to the family of the Honorable William R. "Bo" Brown, Representative of House District 70, and to the members of the Capitol Press Corps.
